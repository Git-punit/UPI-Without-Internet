Mesh simulator

Simulates a payment travelling phone to phone with no internet until a "bridge" phone gets online and uploads it. The phones are simulated, but the crypto and the backend are real. The sender's key is generated in this browser, the packet is signed and encrypted here in the same format the Android app uses, and the backend verifies it exactly as it would a phone's. ← Dashboard

Log in on the dashboard first, then come back.

1. Sender phone (offline)

No phone yet. Registering needs internet; after that, paying works offline.

2. The mesh

Bluetooth range: sender ↔ stranger-1 ↔ stranger-2 ↔ stranger-3 ↔ bridge-A, and stranger-2 ↔ bridge-B. Each gossip round, every phone copies its packets to the phones in range. A packet stops spreading after 5 hops.
